Pensions

Your pension, finally in your pocket

A working prototype of the redesigned FCMB Pensions app, built on the new brand: the Living Flow, calmer type and clearer money.

  1. Watch the app open, then log in. Any details work.
  2. Check the balance, recent contributions and fund price on Home.
  3. Generate a statement, then try the pension calculator.
  4. Ask live chat a question, then log out and back in with Face ID.

All names, balances and messages are sample data.
